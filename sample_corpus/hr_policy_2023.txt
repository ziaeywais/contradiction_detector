EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — HUMAN RESOURCES POLICIES
Acme Corporation | Version 4.2 | Effective January 1, 2023

SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT RELATIONSHIP

Acme Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. All employment decisions are made without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, genetic information, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local law.

Employment at Acme Corporation is at-will, meaning either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, with or without notice, subject to applicable law.

SECTION 2: WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday, totaling 40 hours per week. Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance and to be punctual.

Employees must obtain approval from their direct manager before taking any unscheduled time off. Excessive absenteeism or tardiness may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

SECTION 3: PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

3.1 Vacation Accrual

Full-time employees accrue paid vacation at a rate of 1.25 days per calendar month, resulting in 15 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Part-time employees who work at least 20 hours per week accrue vacation on a pro-rated basis.

New employees begin accruing vacation from their first day of employment but may not use vacation time until they have completed 90 days of continuous service.

3.2 Vacation Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the following calendar year. Any vacation days in excess of 5 days that remain unused at the end of the calendar year will be forfeited and cannot be carried over, paid out, or otherwise compensated. Employees are strongly encouraged to use their vacation time throughout the year.

3.3 Vacation Scheduling

Vacation requests must be submitted to the employee's direct manager at least two weeks in advance for periods of one to three days. For vacation periods exceeding three consecutive days, employees must submit requests at least four weeks in advance. Vacation scheduling is subject to business needs and manager approval.

3.4 Sick Leave

Employees accrue 1 sick day per month, up to a maximum of 12 sick days per year. Unused sick leave does not carry over from year to year and cannot be converted to vacation time or cash compensation.

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY

4.1 Eligibility

Remote work arrangements are available to employees whose job duties are conducive to working outside the office. Eligibility is determined by the employee's manager in consultation with Human Resources.

4.2 Remote Work Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week, provided they maintain consistent communication with their team and are available for all scheduled meetings. Employees working remotely are expected to be available during standard business hours (9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in their local time zone).

Employees must be present in the office a minimum of two days per week to facilitate in-person collaboration and maintain team cohesion.

4.3 Equipment and Security

The company will provide a laptop computer for remote work. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure internet connection and ensuring that company data is not accessible to unauthorized individuals. Personal devices may not be used to access company systems without prior written approval from IT Security.

SECTION 5: PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

5.1 Code of Ethics

All employees are expected to conduct themselves in a professional manner at all times and to adhere to the company's Code of Ethics. The Code of Ethics is available on the company intranet and is incorporated by reference into this handbook.

5.2 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager and Human Resources. A conflict of interest exists when an employee's personal, financial, or other interests could impair — or appear to impair — their ability to act in the best interests of the company.

SECTION 6: SEPARATION OF EMPLOYMENT

6.1 Voluntary Resignation

Employees wishing to resign from the company should provide a minimum of two weeks' written notice to their direct manager and Human Resources. While the company prefers two weeks' notice, employees are reminded that employment is at-will and no minimum notice is legally required except where required by contract.

6.2 Final Pay

Final paychecks will be issued on the next regular pay date following the last day of employment, or earlier if required by applicable state law. Any accrued but unused vacation will be paid out in the final paycheck.

SECTION 7: BENEFITS

7.1 Health Insurance

The company offers comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance beginning on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. The company pays 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage.

7.2 Retirement Plan

Acme Corporation offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan. Employees become eligible to participate after 90 days of employment. The company matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of the employee's annual salary.

7.3 Parental Leave

Employees who have been employed for at least 12 months are eligible for up to 8 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

SECTION 8: TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

The company is committed to the professional development of all employees. Employees may request up to $1,500 per year in tuition reimbursement for job-related education and training programs, subject to manager and HR approval.

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Employees are required to sign and return the attached acknowledgment form confirming receipt and review of this handbook. Questions about any policies in this handbook should be directed to Human Resources at hr@acmecorp.com.

This handbook is not a contract of employment. The company reserves the right to amend, modify, or revoke any policy at any time, with or without notice.
