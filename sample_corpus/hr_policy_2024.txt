EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — HUMAN RESOURCES POLICIES
Acme Corporation | Version 5.0 | Effective January 1, 2024

SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT RELATIONSHIP

Acme Corporation is an equal opportunity employer committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace. All employment decisions are made without regard to any characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local law.

Employment at Acme Corporation is at-will unless otherwise specified in a written employment agreement signed by an authorized officer of the company.

SECTION 2: WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday, totaling 40 hours per week. The company also offers a flexible start window between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM for employees whose managers have approved flexible scheduling.

SECTION 3: PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

3.1 Vacation Entitlement

Effective January 1, 2024, all full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, regardless of tenure. This change reflects Acme's commitment to employee well-being and competitive compensation. Part-time employees who work at least 20 hours per week receive vacation on a pro-rated basis relative to a 40-hour standard week.

New employees begin accruing vacation from their first day of employment but may not use vacation time until completing 60 days of continuous service.

3.2 Vacation Carryover Policy

Effective January 1, 2024, unused vacation days may not be carried over into the following calendar year. Employees are expected to use all vacation days within the calendar year in which they are earned. Any vacation days unused at year-end will be forfeited. This policy encourages employees to take regular rest and promotes a healthy work-life balance.

3.3 Vacation Scheduling

Vacation requests must be submitted to the employee's direct manager at least two weeks in advance for periods of one to three days. For vacation periods exceeding three consecutive days, employees must submit requests at least four weeks in advance. All vacation scheduling is subject to business needs and manager approval.

3.4 Sick Leave

Employees accrue 1.5 sick days per month, up to a maximum of 18 sick days per year. Unused sick leave may be carried over year to year, up to a maximum bank of 30 days.

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY

4.1 Hybrid-First Culture

Acme Corporation is committed to a fully flexible work environment. As of January 1, 2024, all eligible employees are permitted to work fully remotely, subject to the requirements in this section.

4.2 Remote Work Requirements

All remote employees must maintain a dedicated workspace free from distractions and must be available during core business hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the company's Pacific Time zone). Employees choosing to work from the office are always welcome to do so.

There is no minimum in-office attendance requirement. Managers may schedule periodic team on-sites, typically quarterly, with at least 30 days advance notice.

4.3 Equipment and Security

The company will provide a laptop computer, external monitor, and ergonomic keyboard/mouse set for remote work. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure internet connection. An approved VPN must be active at all times when accessing company systems remotely.

SECTION 5: PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

5.1 Code of Ethics

All employees are expected to conduct themselves in a professional manner and to adhere to the company's Code of Ethics and Compliance Program. Both documents are available on the company intranet.

5.2 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest on an annual basis using the online disclosure form. Disclosures are reviewed by the Compliance Committee.

SECTION 6: SEPARATION OF EMPLOYMENT

6.1 Voluntary Resignation

Employees wishing to resign must provide a minimum of four weeks' written notice to their direct manager and Human Resources. This extended notice period ensures orderly transition of responsibilities. The company will endeavor to accept early departures in cases of documented hardship.

6.2 Final Pay

Final paychecks will be issued on the next regular pay date following the last day of employment, or earlier as required by applicable state law. Accrued but unused vacation will not be paid out upon separation, consistent with the use-it-or-lose-it vacation policy.

SECTION 7: BENEFITS

7.1 Health Insurance

The company offers comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance beginning on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. The company pays 85% of the premium for employee-only coverage, reflecting an increase from prior years.

7.2 Retirement Plan

Acme Corporation offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan. Employees become eligible to participate on their first day of employment, with no waiting period. The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of the employee's annual salary.

7.3 Parental Leave

Employees who have been employed for at least 6 months are eligible for up to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

SECTION 8: TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

The company is committed to professional development. Employees may request up to $3,000 per year in tuition reimbursement for job-related education and training programs, subject to manager and HR approval.

ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Employees are required to complete the digital acknowledgment in the HR portal confirming receipt and review of this handbook. Questions should be directed to Human Resources at hr@acmecorp.com or via the HR Help Desk portal.

This handbook supersedes all prior versions. It is not a contract of employment. The company reserves the right to amend any policy at any time.
